# Claim-ledger instructions

- Claim IDs, statuses, evidence classes, sources, and reviews are governed by
  `claims/schema.json` and `docs/claim-status-policy.md`.
- Never allocate, promote, weaken, or delete a claim without the exact
  registered evidence and required audit.
- Definitions, sourced results, proofs, exact computations, reproductions,
  observations, estimates, conjectures, hypotheses, and negative results stay
  distinct.
- Every generated numerical claim cites immutable run evidence. Preserve failed
  verifications and counterboundaries.
- Validate the complete ledger after any change.
