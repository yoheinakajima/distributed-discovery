# TreasureBench Agents v1 provider-outcome policy v3

Status: prospective, frozen, not executed. Authority: AO-0011 and DD-010
engineering only. Policy v3 composes with unchanged protocol-validity policy
v2 only for future campaigns that explicitly register both policies.

It does not rewrite or reclassify any historical policy, schema, campaign,
trace, decision, or lock. AO-0010 and v4 remain permanently quarantined. This
task does not diagnose v4 or infer its hidden provider, route, or exact error.

## Terminal outcomes

Every intended pairing receives one terminal status. A safely retainable
completed response is either `protocol-valid` or `protocol-invalid` under
independent Method C. This includes completed refusals, token-limited
responses, and nonconforming structured responses.

`provider-operational-missing` means the exact direct route produced no
completed usable response after the bounded identical-request transport
attempts because of one prospectively registered availability class.
`provider-contract-or-safety-failure` means a request, schema, model, route,
substitution, hidden-reasoning, credential, authority, identity, retention,
taxonomy, or other safety invariant failed.

Only the provider-specific operational classes in the machine-readable policy
may become ordinary missingness. Unregistered or ambiguous errors quarantine.

## Retry, trace, and circuit breaker

Each logical request has at most two bounded transport attempts and exactly
one schema-only repair. There are zero semantic or outcome-dependent retries,
replacement pairings or batches, silent normalizations, or invalid-output
credits.

An operationally missing pairing stops immediately within that pairing,
retains every safely completed prior turn in one unique terminal missing
trace, creates and credits no action, and remains in every intended
denominator. It is never retried, replaced, spliced, or regenerated.

The engineering circuit breaker quarantines on three consecutive operational
missing pairings for one provider, ten cumulative operational missing
pairings, any contract/safety failure, or a public canary that is not
protocol-valid. It reads terminal status and sequence only. It is not a
scientific or performance threshold and cannot be reset or loosened after live
results.

## All-pairing bounds

Protocol-valid pairings retain exact registered metrics. Protocol-invalid
pairings retain policy-v2 intervals. Operationally missing pairings use the
v3 metric-specific feasible support or task-specific comparator bounds.
Calls, tokens, cost, retry count, provider completion, and provider
missingness remain exact operational quantities.

Missingness is not an invalid submitted action. Protocol compliance is
nonapplicable without a completed response. Operational no-valid-action credit
is recorded separately and is not presented as a known latent performance
point estimate.

Every intended eligible pairing remains in primary architecture-contrast
bounds, and an independent implementation must reproduce all endpoints and
denominators. Valid-output-only and completed-response-only summaries are
secondary, explicitly selection-conditioned diagnostics. No complete-case
unconditional effect, ranking, composite, or leaderboard is permitted.

## Batch disposition

Isolated protocol-invalid and provider-operational-missing outcomes do not
alone quarantine. Engineering completion requires two protocol-valid public
canaries, fresh custody, exactly one allowed terminal status for all 500
pairings, no contract/safety failure or circuit-breaker trigger, complete
identity and correspondence checks, provider closure before lock, zero calls
after lock, lock and commitment verification, post-lock Methods A/B/C,
independent provider classification and all-pairing bounds, and passing
contamination, corruption, redaction, cap, and retained-state checks.

There is no accepted provider-missingness rate. Later informativeness depends
on the width of all-pairing bounds, not rerunning until availability looks
better.
