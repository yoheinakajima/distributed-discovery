requires both public canaries, fresh custody, exactly one unique terminal trace
classified protocol-valid or protocol-invalid for all 500 intended pairings,

or duplicate pairing; response/ledger, trace/inventory, or retained-state

implementation disagreement; or unsafe retained state.
