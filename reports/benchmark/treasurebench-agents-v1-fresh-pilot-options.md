two-direct-route, one-repeat sealed engineering pilot: 500 private runs under

proposed USD 6 cap, but it cannot replace the complete matrix confirmation. A
provider-route replacement remains available only after a separate route
audit, preflight, registration, and new batch. Deferral costs nothing and
leaves readiness unconfirmed.
