## Authority and independence

The Distributed Discovery repository is the sole authority for manuscript,
claim, proof, run, and evidence state. Research Atlas coordinates exact
pointers, review status, reviewer provenance, and the complete handoff; it does
not supply or alter scientific truth.

Complete the review without seeing ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok's other
reviews. Do not edit the manuscript. Return findings only after checking the
exact packet identity. Atlas will hold each result until all four independent
reviews are complete, then return one simultaneous provenance-bound bundle.

## Review request

Assess:

1. theorem and proof correctness;
2. claim status, stated scope, qualifiers, and negative-result boundaries;
3. whether citations support the exact nearby statements and whether a
   load-bearing neighboring literature is missing;
4. self-containment and dependency clarity;
5. exposition, organization, definitions, and reader comprehension;
6. figures, tables, captions, and visual legibility; and
7. limitations, failed universal claims, and counterboundaries.

For each finding, return the fields in the machine-readable packet. Cite a
manuscript location and the affected claim or citation when applicable.
Distinguish a demonstrated error from a question, preference, missing
explanation, or request for new evidence. Do not invent a repository claim,
proof, run, citation, result, or publication status.

## Boundary

This is review of a working paper, not peer review, submission, publication,
release, DOI assignment, or Explore Science upload. Information Sharing
Frontier is outside scope. No provider API call, spend, new scientific
execution, or manuscript merge is authorized by this packet.
