# Paper instructions

- Treat a paper as a theorem-family editorial unit, not a study or evidence
  source.
- Resolve every scientific statement through registered claim/study/run
  authority and preserve qualifiers, counterboundaries, nulls, and failures.
- Follow paper ownership, dependency, lifecycle, citation, licensing, and
  self-containment records in `docs/`.
- Use deterministic builders and source-generated data. Never hand-edit a
  generated PDF or displayed number.
- Run the relevant paper build, citation/provenance checks, byte-reproduction
  checks, and all-page visual QA for changed artifacts.
- A build, release, DOI, or site deployment does not promote scientific or
  publication status.
