# Study instructions

- A registered study is the evidence unit. Read its `README.md`, `plan.md`, and
  `status.yml` before action.
- Freeze the model, configuration, resources, evidence class, independent
  method, corruptions, stopping outcomes, and non-goals before execution.
- Execute only the registered configuration from clean committed source and
  preserve null, negative, failed, and partial certified outcomes.
- Update study status, result index, claims, and living plans together after
  material findings.
- A registration profile or task contract cannot by itself authorize a
  scientific run or claim promotion.
